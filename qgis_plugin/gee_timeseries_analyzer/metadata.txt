about=Ports two Google Earth Engine Code Editor apps (a time series analyzer and a field inspector) to a QGIS plugin. Draw any number of polygons on the QGIS canvas, import them from a shapefile / GeoPackage / any polygon layer, or click a field of a polygon layer (or of a GEE FeatureCollection asset downloaded as a crop-styled layer) to inspect it immediately. For a chosen year or date range the plugin extracts, in one server-side reduceRegions per product: Sentinel-1 GRD VV and VH backscatter (descending and ascending, border-noise masked), a Sentinel-2 SR optical index (SCL cloud mask) and a Landsat 8/9 C2 L2 optical index (QA_PIXEL cloud mask) -- NDVI, EVI, SAVI, NDWI or NDMI. Results are shown as combined (all polygons) and per-polygon matplotlib charts with a re-adjustable valid-pixel threshold, exportable as CSV and PNG. Monthly Sentinel-2 median composites (default May, July, September; true colour, false colour or NDVI) are added as map layers. Also registered as Processing algorithms. Requires the "Google Earth Engine" QGIS plugin (provides earthengine-api and the sign-in) and pandas + matplotlib (bundled with QGIS on Windows).
